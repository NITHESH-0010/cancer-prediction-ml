Understanding Cancer Risk

Major Causes & Factors

Cancer is primarily driven by genetic mutations, which can be inherited or acquired through lifestyle and environmental factors. Key drivers include tobacco smoke, obesity, and excessive radiation or chemical exposure.

Effects on the Body

Abnormal cells multiply and form tumors that can disrupt organ function and weaken the immune system. Early detection prevents these cells from spreading.

Prevention

Maintaining a healthy weight, exercising regularly, avoiding tobacco, and attending routine medical screenings significantly lower overall risk.

When to see a doctor

If you notice unexplained weight loss, persistent pain, unusual lumps, or changes in bodily habits, consult a healthcare professional. Do not rely solely on this educational tool.
